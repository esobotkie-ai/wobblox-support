Wobblox Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Wobblox is an iPhone puzzle game made by Earl Sobotkiewicz ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

What stays on your device

Wobblox has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress, high scores, Quest stars, Daily puzzle streak, settings (such as sound and haptics) and whether you removed ads are stored only on your device. I do not receive this information, and I do not run any servers that collect it.

Information I collect

I do not collect any personal information through the game myself. The game has no analytics service of my own.

Advertising

Wobblox shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your device identifiers (for example the advertising identifier), IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows a consent form before ads are requested, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • On iOS, the game asks for your permission to track across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you say no, ads may still appear, but they will not be personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • The game also offers an optional ad that you choose to watch in return for a reward (a second chance in a game).

Purchases

Wobblox offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are processed entirely by Apple. I do not receive or store your payment details. I only learn, through Apple's purchase system, that the purchase was made so the game can remove ads.

Children

Wobblox is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Decline the tracking prompt, or turn tracking off in iOS Settings.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop the ads shown between games.
  • Delete the app to remove all data stored by the game on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
