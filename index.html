Wobblox Support

Wobblox is a jelly block puzzle game for iPhone: place pieces on the board, clear rows and columns, and chain combos. Play Endless, Quest levels, or the Daily puzzle.

Need help?

Email lanecraftapp@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version, plus a short description of what happened.

Common questions

How do I remove ads? Open Settings in the game and tap Remove ads. If you bought it before, tap Restore purchase.

Do I lose progress if I delete the app? Progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the app removes it.
